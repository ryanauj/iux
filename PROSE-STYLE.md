# Prose style

Rules for explanatory copy in any text-heavy app (chapter ledes, body
paragraphs, callout bodies). The aim: state the mechanic plainly and let the
reader draw the conclusion. Keep a light voice — do not flatten to a
reference-manual register.

## Keep
- Concrete figures and live data.
- Grounded, specific anecdotes.
- Metaphors that are load-bearing — tied to a real component or a structure
  reused across the app. One such metaphor is an asset; decorative ones are not.

## Cut or limit
1. **Expectation-reversal scaffolding — CUT. This is a structure, not a word
   list.** The banned move: state what a reader would naively expect, then
   reveal the reality. It appears in unlimited surface forms — “looks like the
   simplest move… but one rule turns it into a puzzle,” “clean and workable,
   but it creates a problem,” “you’d guess it’s flat — it isn’t,” “what matters
   most is…,” “the real point is….” Delete the setup clause and state the
   mechanic directly. **Test:** if a clause’s only job is to signal an upcoming
   surprise, or to label what matters rather than state it, cut the clause and
   keep the fact. The phrases above are illustrations, not an exhaustive
   find-list; match paraphrases too.
2. **Antithesis via em-dash — LIMIT.** "not just X — Y" / "less X than Y": at
   most one per page, never in the opening lede. Usually splits into two plain
   sentences.
3. **Dramatized / personified verbs — CUT.** No "shrugged," "stings,"
   "feared," "happily," "punishing," "the league's answer." Use neutral verbs
   that name the mechanism: costs, triggers, rises, removes, restricts, applies.
4. **Decorative extended metaphor — CUT.** A metaphor must map to something
   real and recur consistently, or it goes.
5. **Punchy sentence fragments as section openers — LIMIT.** Fine once;
   convert to full sentences if more than one section per app opens this way.

## Person
Default to third person ("a team," "an owner") in explanatory prose. Reserve
second person ("you") for copy that addresses the reader operating an
interactive tool. The goal is one consistent rule, applied app-wide.
